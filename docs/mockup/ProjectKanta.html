Kanta

36 shots, then you wait. A film camera for your phone, with the waiting kept in on purpose.

Source★ 48 · updated 3d ago
Result

About 1,200 rolls developed by 80 or so people. Shots per person went down. Photos people actually kept went up. That was the whole point.

The problem

Phone cameras make it free to take 40 photos of the same plate of noodles, and nobody looks at them again. I wanted the disposable-camera feeling back: a limited roll, no preview, a wait before you see anything. Not because it is better, because it makes you care.

What I built

A small app and a FastAPI backend. Each roll is 36 exposures. When you finish, the roll goes to a Lambda that applies the film look and a slow develop, then lands in Azure blob storage. The app only ever shows a roll after it is developed.

Why Lambda instead of the phone

The first version processed film on-device. It killed the battery and the look drifted between Android and iOS. Moving it server-side made every roll identical and let me change the recipe without shipping an app update. The trade is latency, which here was free, because waiting is the product.

How I know it works

Every roll writes a timing record. Median develop is 4 minutes 10 seconds. Failed rolls retry three times and I have only had to fix two by hand.
