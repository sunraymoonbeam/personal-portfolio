Wok hei at home

Why is a page about frying in a software portfolio? Because a wok is a control problem. Heat, timing, moisture, and one variable you cannot buy your way out of. Same job, different kitchen.

The problem is watts

A hawker wok burner runs somewhere around 100,000 BTU. A good home induction hob is closer to 10,000. Wok hei is not a technique you are missing, it is energy you do not have. So the whole game is giving the pan back the heat you keep stealing from it.

What actually moved the needle

  • Carbon steel, not non-stick. It takes and holds a hard sear.
  • Cook in two batches. Everything in at once drops the pan 80°C and you are steaming, not frying.
  • Dry the protein properly. Water is the enemy of the Maillard reaction.
  • Sauce down the hot side of the wok, never into the middle. It caramelises on the way in.
  • Everything prepped before the burner goes on. You get about 90 seconds of usable heat.

What I still cannot do

Real smokiness. That comes from oil igniting above the pan, and my extractor fan has opinions about that. This is the point where the honest answer is that a home kitchen has a ceiling, and I am near it.
