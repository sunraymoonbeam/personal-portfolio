I like full-stack, end to end: the database, the API, the screen, the deploy. AI goes in where it earns its place, not because it is the year for it.

Mostly I like the part before the code. Working out what is actually wrong, who it is wrong for, and what the smallest thing is that would fix it. Then owning that until it works in production, not until the ticket closes.

The most useful thing I have built at work is a tracing dashboard. The most fun is a film camera app that makes you wait four minutes.
