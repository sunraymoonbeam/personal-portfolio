# Interview questions

Questions to ask Zack before writing or rewriting any page. The answers are
the raw material for the "Looking back" sections and for anything else that
needs his own words. Never invent an answer.

## How to run an interview

- Ask **3 to 5 questions at a time**, starting from the page he wants to work
  on. He answers in quick fragments or voice notes, and that's fine.
- Write his answers into the **Answers** block under each section, close to
  his words, with the date. Then draft the copy following
  [`writing.md`](writing.md) and show it to him before changing the site.
- Skip a question he has already answered (check the Answers block and the
  Google Drive `portfolio/behavioural` notes first).
- When a story makes a good reflection, ask one follow-up for the concrete
  detail: who, what exactly, what happened next.
- Ask about photos at the end of each section. Real photos beat stock.

Priority: **Carro, then AI Singapore**, then the rest.
