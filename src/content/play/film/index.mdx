## How it started

A friend lent me an Olympus point-and-shoot in 2022 and I did not give it back
for four months. The thing that got me was not the look — digital can fake the
look. It was the **constraint**.

## What the constraint does

Thirty-six frames. No screen. You will not see any of it for a week. So you
stand there for a moment longer and ask whether this is actually worth a frame,
and most of the time the answer is no.

I take perhaps a tenth as many photos on film. I keep far more of them.

## The part that became a project

I wanted that feeling on the phone that is always in my pocket, which is how
[Kanta](/projects/kanta) happened — a fixed roll, no preview, and a develop step
you cannot skip.

## What I shoot

Portra 400 when I am feeling rich, Gold 200 when I am not. An Olympus XA and a
Pentax K1000 that weighs as much as a brick.
