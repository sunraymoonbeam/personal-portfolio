## The problem is watts

A hawker wok burner runs somewhere around 100,000 BTU. A good home induction hob
is closer to 10,000. Wok hei is not a technique you are missing, it is energy
you do not have. So the whole game is giving the pan back the heat you keep
stealing from it.

## What actually moved the needle

- Carbon steel, not non-stick. It takes and holds a hard sear.
- Cook in **two batches**. Everything at once drops the pan 80°C.
- Dry the protein properly. Water is the enemy of the Maillard reaction.
- Sauce down the hot side of the wok, never into the middle.

## What I still cannot do

Real smokiness. That comes from oil igniting above the pan, and my extractor fan
has opinions about that.
