## Why I keep going back

Climbing is the only hobby I have where the feedback is completely honest. You
either finish the problem or you are on the mat. There is no partial credit and
no way to talk your way through it.

## The part that surprised me

How much of it is reading rather than strength. Beginners — me, for about a
year — treat every problem as "pull harder". The people who are actually good
stand at the bottom for two minutes first, work out where their hips need to be,
and then do the whole thing in one go looking faintly bored.

That is debugging. I spent my first two years of programming pulling harder too.

## Where I am

Comfortable on V3, projecting V4, occasionally humbled by a V2 that happens to
be slabby. Slab is my weakness because slab is about trusting your feet, and I
do not.
