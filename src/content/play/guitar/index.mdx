## The honest version

I have played since 2016 and I am not good. I can hold down a song at a party.
I cannot improvise, my barre chords buzz, and I have restarted learning theory
maybe four times.

## Why it is on here anyway

Because everything else I do rewards getting better, and it is useful to have
one thing that does not. Nobody is reviewing my guitar. There is no roadmap and
no one waiting on it.

I think that is actually why I have kept it for ten years and dropped things I
was better at.
