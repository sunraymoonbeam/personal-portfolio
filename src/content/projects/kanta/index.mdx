## Why I built it

After an event, the photos are spread across a dozen camera rolls and group
chats, and most people never see most of them. I wanted one album that every
guest adds to, where you can jump straight to the photos you're in instead of
scrolling through hundreds.

## How it works

The backend is FastAPI on Azure Container Apps. Image files go to Azure Blob
Storage, and the metadata used to search them goes to Azure PostgreSQL. Face
clustering runs separately as an AWS Lambda job, outside the app that takes
the uploads.

<Figure src={storage} alt="FastAPI on Azure Container Apps connects to Blob Storage for image files and PostgreSQL for queryable photo data." caption="Files in Blob Storage, everything you search by in PostgreSQL." />

## Storage design

Splitting storage this way was the decision that shaped the rest. Blob storage
is cheap for large files but can't answer "which photos is this person in?",
and a database is the reverse.
