## The problem

Phone cameras make it free to take 40 photos of the same plate of noodles, and
nobody looks at them again. I wanted the disposable-camera feeling back: a
limited roll, no preview, a wait before you see anything. Not because it is
better, because it makes you care.

## What I built

A small app and a FastAPI backend. Each roll is 36 exposures. When you finish,
the roll goes to a Lambda that applies the film look and a slow develop, then
lands in blob storage. The app only ever shows a roll after it is developed.

## Why Lambda instead of the phone

The first version processed film on-device. It killed the battery and the look
drifted between Android and iOS. Moving it server-side made every roll
identical and let me change the recipe without shipping an app update. The trade
is latency, which here was free, because waiting is the product.

## How I know it works

Every roll writes a timing record. Median develop is 4 minutes 10 seconds.
Failed rolls retry three times.
