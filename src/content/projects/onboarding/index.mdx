## Why I made it

At AI Singapore, teammates joined with different technical backgrounds. Some fundamental Linux and version-control concepts were unfamiliar to new team members. I found that explaining them clearly also tested whether I understood them myself.

I made onboarding materials and a small repository with an interactive game. It gave people a place to practise commands and Git workflows, rather than only reading instructions.

## What it changed in my approach

I learned to explain technical ideas in smaller steps and give people a way to try them. I also improved our CI/CD checks so that routine validation depended less on someone remembering every step. My résumé reports an 80% reduction in testing and deployment time after those pipeline changes; that figure applies to the broader CI/CD work, not specifically to this exercise.

I do not have an independent measure of onboarding time. The value I can describe here is the tool I built and the way it supported new teammates.
