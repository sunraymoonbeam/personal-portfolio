## Why

Renting managed everything got expensive and boring. Two small servers do the
same work, and I learn more when it breaks.

## What runs on it

Dokploy for deployment, Traefik for routing and certificates, n8n for the
automations, plus a fleet of Telegram agents that each own one job.
