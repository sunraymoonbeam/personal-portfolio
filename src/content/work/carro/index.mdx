## The role

I joined Carro, a used-car marketplace, as an AI engineer in August 2025. A lot
of Carro's work with customers runs through tickets, which the operations teams
in Singapore, Malaysia and Hong Kong work through every day. I work with those
teams to turn their processes into agents.

## What I built

### Ticketing agents

A ticket can be almost anything: a question about a car, an offer to sell one,
a workshop booking. The agents triage each ticket, then carry it through the
next steps, such as creating an inventory record or booking an appointment.
Each workflow started from the ops team's existing process for that kind of
ticket. Together they process more than 60,000 tickets a month.

### An agent platform for each department

The second project is internal. The platform mirrors Carro's departments: each
one has its own agents for work like pulling data and putting reports
together, and agents can hand work to each other. Role-based access controls
decide which users and roles can reach which agents and data.

### Cost and quality

At this volume, agents get expensive fast. I cut our LLM costs by 71% through
harness engineering and by routing each task to the cheapest model that
handles it well.

Cheaper models are only worth it if the agents still follow the SOPs. Before a
change to an agent goes out, an LLM-as-judge pipeline checks its behaviour
against the team's SOPs. That cut SOP deviations by 30%.

### Marketing outreach

I also automated customer engagement and follow-ups for Carro's Singapore
workshops on n8n, which drove 30% revenue growth for the workshops.
