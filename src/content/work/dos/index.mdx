## The project

In mid-2023 I interned at the Department of Statistics, building LLM
prototypes with different departments. GPT-3.5 was new, LangChain had just come
out and people were already calling it bloated. We tried three kinds of tool:
text classification, question answering and report generation.

## What I built

One was a chatbot for the questions SingStat gets again and again, such as what
a statistic means or where the data comes from. Another helped analysts who
prepare quarterly sector outlooks for the Economic Development Board, and who
had to pull together PDFs, news articles and CSV files to do it.

Both used retrieval-augmented generation: find the relevant documents first,
then have the model answer from them.

## Looking back

Most of this is standard now. LangChain, the library everyone called bloated
that summer, runs a lot of production systems today, including some of the
agents I work on at Carro.
