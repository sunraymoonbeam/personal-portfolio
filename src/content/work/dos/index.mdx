## Where the questions came from

During my internship at the Singapore Department of Statistics, I worked with different departments on possible uses for large language models. The tasks included text classification, answering questions and drafting reports. These were prototypes at an early point in the technology's development.

One use case was a chatbot for recurring questions about statistics, such as definitions and data sources. Another supported analysts preparing sector outlooks for the Economic Development Board. They had to bring together PDFs, news articles and CSV files.

## What I tried

I worked with LangChain and GPT-3.5 to retrieve relevant material before generating an answer. That retrieval step was important for both the question-answering and report tasks: the answer had to be grounded in the right source material.

This internship showed me the practical side of text analytics. A useful system begins with the documents people already work with and the question they need to answer. A fluent response alone does not establish that the answer is sound.
