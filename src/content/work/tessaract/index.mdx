## The work

At Tessaract.io, I maintained databases for law and insurance clients. I used Python and PostgreSQL scripts to improve performance, prepare reports and move client data into production systems. My résumé records migrations of more than 300,000 records for over ten law firms.

This was also client-facing work. I spoke with people about migration needs and the workflows they relied on. Technical correctness mattered, but so did explaining what could be delivered by a deadline.

## A lesson in assumptions

On one migration, confidentiality meant we did not receive representative client data until close to delivery. The real files had inconsistent columns, missing values and formatting we had not planned for. We had to clean and revalidate the data late, which caused delays.

I learned to ask for a representative sample early, leave time for unexpected formats and make assumptions visible in the migration plan. When the data is sensitive and the deadline is fixed, discovering its shape at the end is an avoidable risk.
