## The setup

Carro sells and services cars across four countries, and support runs through
tickets. About 60,000 a month. Six ops leads were reading them by hand, sorting
them, and pasting the same twelve replies.

## The wrong turn

I built the first agent in three weeks, straight against the model API, and it
worked in the demo. Then it went live and I could not see anything. Which
tickets it touched, what it cost, why it replied in Malay to a refund request
nobody wrote in Malay.

I spent the next two weeks not on the agent, but on being able to see the agent.

## What I actually did

- Put **LiteLLM** in front of every model call, so swapping providers is config.
- Sent every call to **Langfuse**: cost per ticket, per agent, per model.
- Rebuilt the agents in **LangGraph** as twelve small workflows.
- Ran a cost trial per agent, moving most to cheaper models where evals held.
- Wired **n8n** into the parts that never needed a model at all.

## What changed

Twelve agents now handle about 60,000 tickets a month. The LLM bill dropped
roughly 80%, mostly by not using a frontier model to sort tickets.

## What I'd do differently

Build the tracing first. And write the eval set with the ops leads on day one,
not after the Malay incident.
