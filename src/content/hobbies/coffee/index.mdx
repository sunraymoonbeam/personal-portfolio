For most of my life, coffee meant kopi. Then I started working full time, and
caffeine became less optional.

I bought a V60, and a simple morning drink turned into a daily experiment.
There are more variables than you'd think: the ratio of coffee to water, grind
size, water temperature, how fast you pour and how long it brews. Change one
and the same beans taste completely different. So most mornings I change one
thing, taste the cup and decide what to try the next day. Plenty of mornings
the only result is a worse cup of coffee.

of making filter coffee. Espresso is the next rabbit hole.
