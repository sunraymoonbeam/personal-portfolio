For most of my life, coffee meant kopi. Then I started working full time and,
somehow, caffeine became less optional.

I bought a V60, and what should have been a simple morning drink turned into a
small daily experiment.

Coffee in, water in, coffee out. In theory.

In practice, there are a lot of variables:

1. Coffee to water ratio
2. Grind size
3. Water temperature
4. Pour rate
5. Brew time
6. Brewing method

Change one of them and the same beans can taste completely different.

Every morning becomes a small science experiment. I make a hypothesis, set the
parameters, brew the cup, taste it, then decide what to change the next day.

Sometimes the conclusion is useful.

Sometimes the conclusion is just that I made a worse cup of coffee.

Either way, there is another experiment tomorrow.

different ways of making filter coffee. Espresso is the next rabbit hole.
