For most of my life, coffee meant kopi. Then I started working full time, and
caffeine became less optional.

I bought a V60, and what should have been a simple morning drink turned into a
small daily experiment. In theory it's coffee and water in, coffee out. In
practice there's a lot to get right:

- the coffee-to-water ratio
- grind size
- water temperature
- how fast you pour
- how long it brews
- the brewer itself

Change any one and the same beans taste different. So every morning I make a
guess, change one thing, brew, taste and decide what to change tomorrow. Some
mornings I learn something. Other mornings I've just made worse coffee, which
is also data.

Since the V60 I've tried the AeroPress, immersion brewing and a few other ways
of making filter coffee. Espresso is the next rabbit hole.

the benchmark rather high. Until I get close, I'll keep changing the numbers
every morning.
