My introduction to guitar was slightly backwards. I started on a [guitarrón](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guitarr%C3%B3n_mexicano), the
big bass guitar from mariachi bands, and, more unusually for a guitar, it's
fretless.

<Photos>
  <Shot src={cover} alt="Playing a guitarrón, a large fretless bass guitar, at an outdoor performance." />
</Photos>

Most guitars divide the neck into neat little boxes: put your finger behind
this fret and you get this note. Take the frets away and nothing tells you
where the note is. Playing becomes much more about feel and intuition. You
listen for the note and let your ear guide your hand.

I think about that outside music too. People carry boxes like these around
with them: the right way to do a job, build a career or live a life. The boxes
make things simpler, but it's easy to get caught up in them and stop asking
whether they fit. Without them, you get more freedom to find your own way, and
many more chances to sound terrible.
