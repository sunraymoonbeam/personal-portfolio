I started guitar slightly backwards, on a guitarrón: the big bass guitar from
mariachi bands. It's also fretless.

On most guitars, frets show you exactly where each note is. Put your finger
behind this one and you get this note. Without frets, nothing tells you your
finger is in the right place. Move a few millimetres and the note goes sharp or
flat, so you learn to listen for the note instead of trusting where your hand
thinks it should be. You also get a lot more chances to sound terrible.

These days I mostly play acoustic guitar, which is far more conventional. I'm
not very good. I play because I like picking it up, learning something new and
slowly getting the noise coming out of it closer to the one in my head.
